1. Connect to your records

Credentials are used in this browser session only and are not saved by this page. A static site cannot keep API keys secret.

2. Choose enquiry

Search for the enquiry/case number, select the correct record, then upload its PDF. The existing PDF link will be replaced.

3. Upload an existing PDF

Choose a PDF from your phone and attach it to the selected enquiry/case number. This does not require the photo-to-PDF library.

4. Or take document photos

At least 2 photos are required; up to 3 photos per enquiry. Each photo is automatically resized and compressed to no more than 400 KB before being added to the PDF. Hold the phone above each page and keep all page corners visible.
